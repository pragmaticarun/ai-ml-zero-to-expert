Interactive weekly quiz (12 questions)

Q1.

Write the ridge closed-form solution.

w = (XᵀX + λI)⁻¹Xᵀy (centred data, unpenalised intercept).

Q2.

Which penalty gives exact zeros, and why?

L1 (lasso). Its constant-magnitude slope at zero (the corner of the diamond) pins weak weights at exactly 0.

Q3.

In the twin-feature demo, what did ridge and lasso do?

Ridge split the weight (1.72, 1.308). Lasso kept one (2.931, 0).

Q4.

State the bias-variance decomposition.

E[test MSE] = bias² + variance + σ².

Q5.

In the Day 32 run, what was degree 9's variance without and with ridge α=1e-3?

0.1544 → 0.0195.

Q6.

What does a large train/val gap mean, and what do you try first?

High variance. Try more data, more regularisation or a simpler model.

Q7.

Why is best_score_ from a grid search optimistic, and what fixes it?

It's the max of noisy estimates (a selection effect). Nested CV, or a held-out test set, fixes it.

Q8.

What caused one CV fold to score R² −0.07 on California with raw polynomial features?

Heavy-tailed features (AveOccup up to about 600) squared by the polynomial expansion. log1p fixed it.

Q9.

Write the softmax function and its stable form.

softmax(z)ₖ = e^{zₖ}/Σⱼ e^{zⱼ}. Stable form: subtract max(z) first.

Q10.

Gradient of softmax cross-entropy with respect to the logits?

P − Y (divided by m for the mean loss).

Q11.

Expected loss at initialisation for 10 classes?

ln 10 ≈ 2.3026.

Q12.

What test MAE did the tuned HistGB reach on Day 35, and why are capped rows harder?

0.2971 ($100k). Their true values are above the 5.0 cap and unknown, so the model under-predicts them (MAE 0.62).

Week overview, goals, project and quiz text

Week goals

  1. Package your Month-1 code into a small reusable library (code/week-05/mlscratch.py).
  2. Benchmark your from-scratch learners against scikit-learn on accuracy and wall time, and optionally against a Rust kernel called from Python through PyO3.
  3. Pass the Month-1 checkpoint: self-graded coding exercises, a written exam, and a review of weak spots.
  4. (Days 31-35) Control overfitting with ridge, lasso and elastic net, and measure bias and variance directly.
  5. Select models honestly with cross-validation, grid/random search and nested CV, and extend classification to many classes with softmax.
  6. Ship a CV-selected California housing model with one honest test number and an error analysis.
