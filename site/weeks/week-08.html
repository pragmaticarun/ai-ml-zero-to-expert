Interactive weekly quiz (12 questions)

Q1.

How do you get PCA components and their variances from the SVD?

Centre X, then Xc = UΣVᵀ. The components are the rows of Vᵀ, and the variances are σ²/(m − 1).

Q2.

What did the PCA reconstruction error equal in our run?

The sum of the dropped eigenvalues divided by 64 (e.g. 4.914 at k=10).

Q3.

Name two things t-SNE plots do not preserve.

Between-cluster distances, cluster sizes (also axes and density).

Q4.

Why did the multivariate Gaussian beat independent Gaussians on Day 51?

The features are strongly correlated, and only the full covariance models that.

Q5.

Write the biased MF prediction formula.

r̂ = μ + bᵤ + bᵢ + pᵤ·qᵢ.

Q6.

What did unregularised MF's test RMSE do between epochs 10 and 30?

It rose from 0.9524 to 1.0173 (overfitting).

Q7.

Why does y.rolling(24).mean() leak, and what is the fix?

The window includes yₜ itself. Fix: y.shift(1).rolling(24).mean().

Q8.

Shuffled vs time-split MAE for calendar + weather features?

26.5 (shuffled) vs 48.7 (time split).

Q9.

Two calibration methods, and when to use each.

Platt/sigmoid for small data and S-shaped distortion. Isotonic for more data and arbitrary monotone distortion.

Q10.

LinearSVC and logreg had the same AUC. What did Platt scaling change?

Brier went from 0.1142 to 0.0777 (calibration). AUC was unchanged.

Q11.

Define avoidable bias and variance (Ng style).

Avoidable bias = train error − reference error. Variance = dev error − train error.

Q12.

In the Rust project, which implementation won the assignment step, and which won the full Lloyd loop?

Rust + rayon won the assignment step (8.3 ms). scikit-learn won the full loop (0.22 s vs Rust 0.70 s).

Week overview, goals, project and quiz text

Status: written in full. Titles match SYLLABUS.md.

Week goals

  1. Compress data with PCA (built from the SVD), and visualise it with t-SNE, knowing what each view preserves.
  2. Detect anomalies with density models and isolation forests, with thresholds tuned on validation data.
  3. Build a recommender from biases plus matrix factorisation on MovieLens 100K.
  4. Forecast with lag features without leaking the future.
  5. Check whether probabilities mean what they say, and explain effects with partial dependence.
  6. Diagnose a model systematically (learning curves, error analysis) and decide what to try next.
  7. Write a Rust/PyO3 k-means kernel and benchmark it honestly against NumPy and scikit-learn.
