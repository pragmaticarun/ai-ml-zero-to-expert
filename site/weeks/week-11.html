Interactive weekly quiz (12 questions)

Q1.

Write the convolution output-size formula and compute it for n = 32, k = 5, p = 2, s = 2.

⌊(n + 2p − k)/s⌋ + 1 = 16.

Q2.

How many parameters does Conv2d(3, 16, 3) have, and how many would the equivalent dense layer need?

448; 44,251,200.

Q3.

Give the timings for loops vs im2col vs torch from Day 71.

Roughly 208–244 ms, 2.1–3.1 ms, 0.27–0.39 ms across reruns (timings depend on machine load).

Q4.

At which depth did the residual net beat the plain one on Day 72, and by how much?

Depth 10: 0.7145 vs 0.6120, about +0.10.

Q5.

Why did the MLP on CIFAR-10-small overfit more than the CNN?

No weight sharing or locality: 1.58M parameters each tied to a pixel position, so it memorises positions.

Q6.

What happened when augmentation was added on Day 73, in train and val accuracy?

Train acc fell from 0.9372 to 0.7928, val acc from 0.7444 to 0.7268 in 10 epochs (smaller gap, no gain yet).

Q7.

Give the three transfer-learning results on Day 74.

From scratch 0.3852, frozen features + LR 0.6424, fine-tune layer4+fc 0.6856.

Q8.

What initial loss do you expect for 10 classes, and what did Day 75 measure?

ln 10 ≈ 2.3026; measured 2.2930.

Q9.

What did the shuffled-label check show, and what would a failure have meant?

Test accuracy fell to 0.0889 (chance). A high value would mean leakage.

Q10.

For fs 8,000 Hz and n_fft 256, give the bin width and number of bins.

31.25 Hz, 129 bins.

Q11.

Which four Fashion-MNIST pairs caused 58% of the Day-77 errors?

T-shirt/top–Shirt, Coat–Shirt, Pullover–Coat, Pullover–Shirt.

Q12.

Define ECE and give the Day-77 value.

Confidence-binned average of |accuracy − confidence|, weighted by bin size; 0.0127.

Week overview, goals, project and quiz text

Status: written in full (Phase 3). Titles match SYLLABUS.md.

Week goals

  • Compute a convolution by hand, with loops and as an im2col matrix multiply, and get the output-size arithmetic right every time.
  • Know the architectural ideas from LeNet to ResNet, and show in a run why residual connections help depth.
  • Train a CNN on CIFAR-10-small on a CPU and compare it fairly with an MLP.
  • Use a pretrained network: frozen features vs fine-tuning.
  • Debug deep nets with a fixed checklist (initial loss, overfit one batch, sanity checks).
  • Turn audio into images (spectrograms) and reuse what you know about CNNs.
  • Ship a classifier with an error analysis (weekly project).

Compute note (honest). All runs are CPU-only (8 threads, no GPU). We therefore use CIFAR-10-small, 20,000 32×32 images, and short training (5-10 epochs). The accuracies below are far below what the same architectures reach on full CIFAR-10 with a GPU and long schedules. They are good for comparisons inside this course, not for comparing with papers.

Datasets: CIFAR-10-small from OpenML, dataset 40926 (https://www.openml.org/d/40926), loaded by load_cifar_small() in code/common.py and split once in code/week-11/_cifar.py into 15,000 train / 2,500 validation / 2,500 test (seed 0), normalised with training statistics. (The original Toronto tarball was unreachable from this box when the course was built; see data/README.md.) Fashion-MNIST via torchvision as in Week 10. ImageNet ResNet-18 weights from https://download.pytorch.org/models/resnet18-f37072fd.pth (about 45 MB). Day 76 generates synthetic audio, so nothing is downloaded.
