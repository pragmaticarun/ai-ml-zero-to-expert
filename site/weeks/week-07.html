Interactive weekly quiz (12 questions)

Q1.

Name one flaw each of MDI and permutation importance.

MDI is computed on training data and biased towards many-split-point features. Permutation importance splits credit badly between correlated features.

Q2.

State Shapley efficiency, and give our row-0 sum.

The values sum to f(x) − f(baseline). Row 0's sum was 0.1379.

Q3.

Shapley values for f = x₁x₂, baseline (0, 0), x = (1, 1)?

0.5 and 0.5.

Q4.

Which hour encoding gave ridge its best bike MAE, and what was it?

One-hot × workingday, MAE 72.6.

Q5.

Why must target encoding be cross-fitted?

Otherwise each row's own label leaks into its feature.

Q6.

At 2% positives, what does "always negative" score on accuracy and PR-AUC?

Accuracy 0.978. PR-AUC about 0.022 (the base rate).

Q7.

What threshold did validation tuning pick on Day 45, and what F1 did it give?

0.0756, giving F1 0.208.

Q8.

On pure noise, what were the leaky and honest CV accuracies on Day 46?

0.770 (leaky) vs 0.470 (honest).

Q9.

What is k-means++ for, and what inertia range did random init give?

Spreading initial centroids out. Random init ranged from 2680.9 to 20954.7.

Q10.

Which k did silhouette pick on the 4-blob data?

k = 3 (the truth was 4).

Q11.

What does the EM E-step compute?

Responsibilities P(component | xᵢ).

Q12.

What ARI did the wholesale segments reach against Channel?

0.5135.

Week overview, goals, project and quiz text

Status: written in full. Titles match SYLLABUS.md.

Week goals

  1. Explain a model honestly: impurity vs permutation importance, and exact Shapley values computed by brute force.
  2. Turn raw columns (hours, categories, text) into features a model can use, and measure which encoding helps.
  3. Handle rare positives with the right metrics, class weights and a threshold chosen on validation data.
  4. Make leakage impossible with Pipeline + ColumnTransformer, and prove the leak exists when you don't.
  5. Begin unsupervised learning: k-means from scratch, then hierarchical clustering, DBSCAN and Gaussian mixtures via EM.
  6. Segment real wholesale customers and validate the segments without labels.
flowchart LR

New datasets: bike sharing (hourly rentals in Washington DC, 2011-2012, 17,379 rows, OpenML id 42712) and wholesale customers (440 clients of a Portuguese distributor, annual spend in 6 categories, UCI). Both are fetched by common.py loaders; URLs are in data/README.md.
