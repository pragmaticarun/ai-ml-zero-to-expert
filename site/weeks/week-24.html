Interactive weekly quiz (12 questions)

Q1.

On Day 162, what were the Male/Female selection rates and DI at t=0.5, and what were the base rates?

Sel 0.257 / 0.073, DI 0.29; base rates 0.304 / 0.111.

Q2.

Why did dropping the sex feature barely change DI?

Other features proxy sex (AUC 0.933); DI moved only 0.29→0.30.

Q3.

State the impossibility result that unequal base rates force, in one sentence.

An imperfect classifier cannot simultaneously satisfy equal precision (or calibration) and equalised odds when base rates differ.

Q4.

On Day 163, what happened to Female precision under reweighing, and under demographic-parity thresholds?

Reweighing: Female precision 0.741→0.600. DP thresholds: Female precision 0.488.

Q5.

Why must group thresholds be chosen on a train-side split?

So the fairness target is not fit on the same data you report; test remains an honest estimate.

Q6.

Name the top-3 permutation-importance features on Day 164, and the top-3 Shapley features for the highest-score row.

Permutation: marital-status, capital-gain, education-num. Shapley (top row): capital-gain, marital-status, education-num.

Q7.

What ε did Opacus report for DP-SGD at σ=1.0 (δ=1e-5), and what was the test accuracy?

ε ≈ 1.436 (Opacus RDPAccountant); test acc 0.8481.

Q8.

Compare MIA AUC for the Adult LR, the overfit MLP, and DP-SGD σ=1.0.

Adult LR 0.4989; overfit MLP 0.5678; DP-SGD σ=1.0 0.4981.

Q9.

On Day 166, what were clean, FGSM@8/255, PGD@8/255, and random-sign@8/255 accuracies?

Clean 0.7220; FGSM 0.0420; PGD 0.0000; random-sign 0.6720.

Q10.

What was the age-shift accuracy drop (train <40 → test ≥40), and the domain-classifier AUC with age hidden?

Acc 0.8954 → 0.6945; domain AUC 0.800 (random-split control 0.498).

Q11.

On Day 167, what were refusal, over-refusal, and injection-canary results for SmolLM2-135M-Instruct?

Refused 1/3 harmful; over-refusal 0/3 benign; canary did not fire (answered 2+2=4).

Q12.

What go/no-go did Day 168 record, and which mitigation did it prefer for TPR parity?

Course artefact YES, real decision system NO; prefer equal-opportunity group thresholds for TPR parity.

Week overview, goals, project and quiz text

Status: Days 162-168 are written in full. This is Month 6's responsible-AI and safety week: fairness metrics and trade-offs, bias mitigation, interpretability, differential privacy and membership inference, adversarial robustness and distribution shift, LLM safety plus model cards and governance from verified official sources, and a weekly project that audits one earlier model with a written report. Every number comes from code/week-24/day162_*.py to day168_*.py, each run on this box, with logs in code/week-24/out/. Shared helpers (Adult loader, logistic-regression pipeline, group metrics, Month 3 CNN loader) are in code/week-24/_dep.py.

Retitled from the original SYLLABUS rows. The rows for 162-164 were deployment leftovers (QAT/pruning/distillation, llama.cpp tokens/sec, real-time multimedia). Those topics were covered in Week 23 (Days 156-157, 159). This week follows the responsible-AI brief instead. The SYLLABUS rows are updated to match. Days 165-168 already named fairness, privacy/security, explainability/docs and the audit project; they are kept and expanded.

The dataset and models. Fairness, mitigation, interpretability (tabular), privacy and the Day 168 audit use Adult (OpenML adult version 2 / UCI Adult Census Income), already cached via data/download.py and common.load_adult. A 70/30 stratified split (seed 0) gives 34,189 train and 14,653 test rows; P(>50K) on test is 0.239, and the Female share is 0.335. The audited protected attribute is sex (Female = 1). The model is a scikit-learn LogisticRegression (C=1, max_iter=2000) behind OneHotEncoder + StandardScaler. Adversarial and CNN interpretability reuse the Month 3 CIFAR-10-small CNN (Day 73, weights in code/week-11/out/day73_cnn.pt). LLM safety uses SmolLM2-135M-Instruct from the local Hugging Face cache.

Not available, and labelled wherever it appears: - No RAPL / power meter. Energy figures use measured wall-clock times multiplied by an ASSUMED 45 W package draw. Every joule/kWh figure is labelled ASSUMED. - No legal advice. NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act notes come from pages/PDFs opened this session (NIST.AI.100-1.pdf; the Commission AI Act overview). They are not compliance guidance. - Opacus 1.6.0 was installed for a cross-check of the RDP accountant only; DP-SGD itself is implemented from scratch in Day 165.

Compute note. CPU-only 8-core Xeon, ~15 GB RAM. Adult logistic regression fits in under a second. The CNN adversarial loop and SmolLM2 greedy decode are the slowest steps (~few seconds each). A GPU would change Day 166 (faster PGD) and Day 167 (faster generation) but would not change any fairness or privacy arithmetic.

Sources (opened and checked this week; links in docs/resources.md): OpenML Adult / UCI; NIST AI RMF 1.0 (NIST.AI.100-1); EU Commission AI Act overview page for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689; Opacus RDPAccountant; Week 21 Day 149 injection-guard numbers (restated, not re-run). Explanations here are original, not copied.
