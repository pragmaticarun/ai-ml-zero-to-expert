Day 30: Written exam, closed book (1h15)

Day 30 · 12 questions parsed from weeks/week-05.md · Day 30 page

Q1.

A float32 array of shape (1000, 256). What are its bytes and strides?

1000·256·4 = 1,024,000 bytes. Strides (1024, 4).

Q2.

Why is X @ w faster than a Python loop of dot products? Give two reasons.

Compiled, SIMD, cache-friendly BLAS kernels, and no per-element interpreter overhead (plus multithreading).

Q3.

Name two ways information can leak from test to train, and how to prevent each.

Fitting the scaler/encoder on all the data (fit on train only), and target-derived features such as price_per_sqft (drop them). Also duplicate or grouped entities across splits (use a group split).

Q4.

Cosine similarity of (1, 2, 2) and (2, 1, 2)?

(2 + 2 + 4)/(3·3) = 8/9 ≈ 0.889.

Q5.

Why prefer lstsq/QR over the normal equation with an explicit inverse?

The normal equation squares the condition number, and inverting is slower and less accurate (Hilbert example: error 0.28 vs 5.7).

Q6.

Explain PCA as an SVD in 3 sentences. What does "explained variance" mean?

Centre X, then X = UΣVᵀ. The rows of Vᵀ are directions of maximal variance, and the scores are XV. Explained variance of component i = σᵢ²/Σσⱼ².

Q7.

Derive the gradient of J = (1/2m)‖Xw − y‖².

∇J = Xᵀ(Xw − y)/m, by the chain rule on the squared norm.

Q8.

What is the stable learning-rate bound for a quadratic with Hessian eigenvalues 1 and 50, and how many times slower is progress along the flat direction?

α < 2/50 = 0.04. The flat direction contracts by (1 − α·1) per step vs the steep one's (1 − α·50), so it is about κ = 50 times slower.

Q9.

Prevalence 2%, sensitivity 90%, FPR 10%. What is P(condition | +)?

0.018/(0.018 + 0.098) = 0.155.

Q10.

Why is MSE the MLE under Gaussian noise, and log-loss the MLE for Bernoulli labels?

The Gaussian log-likelihood is −Σ(y − ŷ)²/(2σ²) + const. The Bernoulli log-likelihood is Σ[y log p + (1 − y) log(1 − p)].

Q11.

Train error 1%, val error 12%. Name the diagnosis and three remedies.

Overfitting (variance). More data, regularisation, a simpler model or fewer features (early stopping too).

Q12.

At which threshold would you deploy the Day-27 cancer model, and why?

Probably t ≈ 0.1 (recall 1.0, with 11 false positives out of 143) in screening, where missing cancer is costlier. Choose it on validation data, and confirm on test only once.
